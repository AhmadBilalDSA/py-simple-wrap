# Code of Conduct 🌱
 
## Our Pledge
 
`py-simple-wrap` exists to make Python feel a little less intimidating, especially for people who are just starting out. That only works if this stays a space where people feel safe asking "dumb" questions, submitting imperfect code, and learning in public.
 
We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
 
## What This Looks Like Day to Day
 
**Examples of behavior that helps this community thrive:**
- Being kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors
- Welcoming beginner questions without condescension — there's no such thing as a stupid question here
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on code
- Assuming good intent, and asking for clarification instead of assuming the worst
- Focusing on what's best for the project and the people learning from it
**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Trolling, deliberately unhelpful "gotcha" reviews, or mocking someone's skill level
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or learning setting
## Scope
 
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the GitHub repo, Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and the documentation site — and also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public spaces (e.g. posting about `py-simple-wrap` on social media).
 
## Enforcement
 
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a private report to the maintainer, **[@sara-czasak](https://github.com/sara-czasak)**, via GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and safety will be respected.
 
Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. This can include:
 
1. **Correction** — A private, friendly heads-up when something crosses a line unintentionally.
2. **Warning** — A clear warning that the behavior needs to stop, with consequences for continued behavior explained.
3. **Temporary Ban** — A temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent Ban** — A permanent ban for repeated or severe violations, including harassment or targeted aggression.
## Attribution
 
This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1, with the tone adjusted to fit `py-simple-wrap`'s beginner-first spirit.

Thanks for helping keep this a safe, encouraging place to learn. 🌈✨
