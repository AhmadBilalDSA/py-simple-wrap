Why py-simple-wrap exists

Python is famous for being the beginner-friendly language — but "beginner-friendly" still has a learning curve, and a lot of people give up right before it gets fun. py-simple-wrap exists to shorten that gap: a toolbox with everything a beginner needs to build something fun enough, early enough, to want to stick around and go deeper into Python.

It's not trying to replace specialized, production-grade libraries — it's the on-ramp to them, built for the moment before someone's ready to reach for the "real" tool.

That same philosophy applies to the people who build it, not just the people who use it. First contributions can be stressful — especially if you're new to open source, or new to Python altogether. This project is built to make that first step as low-pressure as possible: beginners are genuinely welcome, mistakes are part of learning, and the community grows by helping each other rather than gatekeeping. Teamwork makes the dream work.
